Welcome to Imlil Oasis – Oasis d’Imlil

Tucked away in the charming village of Imlil, at the base of Mount Toubkal, our guesthouse offers a cozy and serene escape from the busy world.

Whether you’re here to explore the majestic mountains or simply relax and reconnect with nature, we are honored to host you.

Meet your host, Lahcen Igdem

Lahcen is a licensed tour guide accredited by the Ministry of Tourism, and the founder of Authentic Toubkal Lodge and Morocco Mountain Guides TC. He knows in depth what the country has to offer.

His goal is to give local and foreign guests the most worthwhile and enriching tour experience, and to make a real contribution to Imlil’s local tourism industry.
